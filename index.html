The problem

What's holding most hotels back

01

Weak Wi-Fi

Outdated access points, dead zones, and no separation between guest and staff networks. The most complained-about issue in hotel reviews, globally.

02

No casting capability

Guests want to stream Netflix, Disney+, and Spotify on the room TV. Most hotels have no casting solution — or one that doesn't reliably work.

03

Outdated TV systems

Legacy IPTV or coaxial systems that can't support modern content, smart features, or casting integration — and are expensive to maintain.

04

Ageing infrastructure

Old cabling, flat network architecture, and no documentation. Everything above depends on this foundation — and most hotels don't know what they're built on.
