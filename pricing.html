Common questions

Pricing FAQ

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes, always. We'll spend 30 minutes on a call understanding your property and what you're dealing with — no charge, no commitment.

Do you charge for travel?

For properties in the North of England, travel is typically included. For sites further afield, we'll agree any travel costs upfront before work begins — no surprises.

Can I start with just an audit?

Absolutely. The connectivity audit is the most common starting point. It gives you a clear picture of what you have and what you need, with no obligation to proceed further with us.

Do you take commissions from vendors?

Never. We have no vendor affiliations and take no referral fees. Every recommendation is made purely in your interest — that's the F.O.G promise.

How long does an audit take?

Most audits are completed in one day on site, with the written report delivered within five working days. Larger or more complex properties may require two days.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. Tell us what's not working and we'll advise on the right starting point — whether that's a full audit or a targeted assessment.
