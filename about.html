"Hotels know their connectivity isn't working. They just don't know who to trust to fix it."

The hospitality technology industry has a problem. The people selling solutions — vendors, resellers, managed service providers — all have a product to push. And most hotel operators don't have the technical knowledge to cut through the noise.

The result is hotels spending money on the wrong equipment, signing contracts they don't fully understand, and still having guests complain about the Wi-Fi six months later.

F.O.G was built to change that. We're independent consultants — we don't sell hardware, we don't take vendor commissions, and we have no referral agreements. Our only job is to understand your property and tell you exactly what it needs.

We started in hospitality connectivity because it's where the gap is biggest and the impact is most immediate. A well-designed network transforms the guest experience, reduces operational headaches, and removes a constant source of negative reviews. It's fixable. We help you fix it.
